Republican Rep. Thomas Massie reintroduced his resolution to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth on Monday, using a procedure that forces the House to act within two legislative days.

Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisThe House has impeached a cabinet member only twice, and Roll Call notes recent forced “privileged” impeachment votes have never passed — so the tally will show how many lame-duck Republicans are willing to break with Trump before Democrats take over Congress in January.
Full story, sources & checks2 sources

Republican Rep. Thomas Massie reintroduced his resolution to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth on Monday, using a procedure that forces the House to act within two legislative days. Roll Call reports a vote could come by Wednesday, though the House could also vote to table the measure or send it to committee. The eight articles allege that Hegseth waged the Iran war in breach of the 1973 War Powers Resolution (three counts), ignored laws meant to limit civilian casualties, oversaw “extrajudicial killings” in strikes on suspected drug boats, retaliated against Sen. NPR says the House may have the votes with all Democrats, Massie and a handful of Republicans, but Senate removal needs a two-thirds vote.

Official proposalCBC News (The Canadian Press) · Nov 9

The federal government is extending the maximum duration of a low-wage Labour Market Impact Assessment — the document employers need to hire a temporary foreign worker — from one year to two.

More1 source
Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisTwo years after Ottawa sharply cut the program, this is a partial reopening aimed at labour shortages in some regions — at a time when national unemployment is 6.7%, so expect a fight over who benefits.

The ban on low-wage hiring in cities with unemployment above 6% stays, but early childhood educators join health care, construction and food processing on the exemption list. Ottawa is also proposing, for March 1, 2027 and only where provinces opt in, to let provinces change the exempt occupations and raise the cap on low-wage temporary workers from 10% to 15% of a workforce. Immigration Minister Lena Diab said jobs should still go to Canadians first. She also announced that the Rural Community Immigration Pilot will become a permanent program.

Note: The March 2027 measures are proposals that depend on provinces opting in.

FDA orders a warning about a rare risk of vision loss on all GLP-1 drugs, including Ozempic, Wegovy and Zepbound

Official statementU.S. Food and Drug Administration, CNBC · Nov 9 (FDA announcement)

The Food and Drug Administration said on Monday it is requiring makers of all GLP-1 medicines to add a warning about a potential increased risk of non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy (NAION), a rare condition that can cause sudden, painless vision loss in one eye, sometimes permanently.

More2 sources
Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisMillions of Americans use these drugs for diabetes and weight loss; the absolute risk is small, but the label change means doctors are now expected to warn patients and act quickly on eye symptoms.

The FDA’s analysis of 126 clinical trials found 6.4 cases per 100,000 patient-years among GLP-1 users versus 3.8 among patients on placebo or other diabetes drugs; observational studies also pointed to higher risk. The agency tells patients to contact a health professional right away about sudden vision changes. Novo Nordisk told CNBC the data “do not establish a cause-and-effect relationship.”

Note: The FDA describes a “potential increased risk”; CNBC notes a causal link has not been established. An AP report on the same decision did not load in our fetch and was not relied on.
