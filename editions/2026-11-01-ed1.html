Last call: NYC early voting closes at 5 p.m. today; Election Day is Tuesday

ReportedUS · Politics & current affairsNov 1, 2026

Today is the ninth and final day of early voting in New York City, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., at your assigned early-voting site, which can differ from your Election Day site. On Tuesday, Nov 3, polls open 6 a.m. to 9 p.m., and anyone in line by 9 p.m. can still vote.

Why it matters
All 435 House seats and 35 Senate seats are on the ballot, deciding whether Republicans keep control of Congress (they hold the Senate 53-47).
Read more· full story & 3 sources

Mail and absentee ballots must be postmarked or dropped off by Nov 3. Nationally, Reuters reports more than 51 million Americans had already voted, citing the University of Florida Election Lab, up from about 46 million before Election Day in 2022.
