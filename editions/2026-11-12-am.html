Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisNvidia makes the chips that power most of the AI boom, so a sudden leadership change at the world’s second most valuable company would ripple through markets — and the spying allegation has turned a corporate scandal into a U.S.–China issue a week before Trump and Xi are due to meet.

Nvidia’s board met late on Wednesday to decide whether to remove co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang, CNBC confirmed, after The Wall Street Journal reported the board was preparing to force him out. By early Thursday no outcome had been announced, and CNBC said the shares were down about 5% in premarket trading after falling more than 9% after hours on Wednesday. Official statementRTHK, The Standard (Hong Kong) · Nov 12

The Legislative Council passed the Northern Metropolis Development Bill on Thursday, the city’s first law dedicated to a single area, letting the government streamline planning, land compensation and other procedures through subsidiary legislation.

More2 sources
Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisThe Northern Metropolis is Hong Kong’s main bet for new homes, tech jobs and integration with Shenzhen; fast-tracking it trades some of the city’s slow, consultative planning process for speed.

The Legislative Council passed the Northern Metropolis Development Bill with government amendments at its third reading on Thursday, the city’s first law dedicated to a single area, RTHK and The Standard report. Secretary for Development Bernadette Linn said the bill passed a month and a half ahead of the government’s schedule, and that subsidiary legislation would go to LegCo in January so measures can take effect in the first quarter of next year. She said the Town Planning Board keeps its role, conservation areas are excluded and noise standards are unchanged.
