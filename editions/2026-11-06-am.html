Finance Minister François-Philippe Champagne tabled Budget 2026 in the Commons on Thursday, pitching nearly $142 billion in new multiyear spending and tax measures aimed at investment, affordability and reducing reliance on the United States. The official document is titled “Masters of Our Destiny.” Department of Finance key-measures materials highlight a Productivity Mega Deduction that would let businesses immediately deduct most new investments, a $25 billion Canada Strong Fund with retail Canada Strong Notes, more than $50 billion toward a national electricity strategy, and over $22 billion over five years in affordability measures including a one-time 50% top-up to the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit for 2026–27. CBC’s live coverage said the budget is the second under Prime Minister Mark Carney and draws criticism from opposition parties even as Liberals frame it as building the economy at home. Champagne’s tabled remarks ask for an order of the day to consider the ways-and-means motions.
