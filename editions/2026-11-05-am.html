Allegation Claimed, charged or argued in court; not proven.

Analysis Interpretation or opinion — the outlet's, an expert's, or Donna Daily's own — marked as such.

Unnamed sources Rests partly on sources the outlet did not name.

State media Relies on state-controlled media (e.g. Xinhua, CCTV, Russian state media).

Labels describe the type of claim a story rests on; they are not a verification grade. Story previews show a label only when it changes how to read the story (an official statement, claim, allegation, analysis, unnamed sources or state media); plain reporting is the default and is stated in each story’s expanded view. Older editions used “Fact”; it is shown as “Reported”. Why it matters is Donna Daily’s own analysis, kept apart from the sourced reporting.
