Official figuresAllegationOffice of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives, NPR · Nov 10 (update to Nov 9 and Nov 10 stories)

Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisThe vote shows House Republicans still close ranks around Trump’s cabinet in their final weeks in the majority — but it only delays the fight, since Democrats take control in January and have promised investigations of Hegseth’s Pentagon.
Full story, sources & checks2 sources

The House voted on Tuesday evening to refer Rep. Thomas Massie’s eight articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, sidestepping a direct vote on impeachment. The House Clerk’s roll call shows the motion passed 211-204: Republicans voted 210-1 in favour (Massie was the lone Republican no), all 203 Democrats who voted opposed it, one independent voted yes and 18 members did not vote. NPR says the vote effectively kills the effort and virtually guarantees it will not be taken up before this Congress ends. Earlier on Tuesday, Hegseth called Massie a “disgruntled and defeated congressman” at a Pentagon news conference and said, “I stand behind every one of these operations.” The articles accused him of waging the Iran war without authorisation, ordering strikes on suspected drug boats, the seizure of Venezuela’s Nicolás Maduro, retaliating against Sen. Mark Kelly and an unlawful campaign in Yemen.

Note: Ongoing story: Donna Daily reported the filing of the articles on Nov. 10 and Hakeem Jeffries’ support on Nov. 9. The articles were allegations; no finding was made on them. NPR notes it remains unclear whether Democrats will try impeachment again next year.

U.S. consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, more than expected, as food and shelter costs climbed

Official figuresU.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, CNBC · Nov 10

The Consumer Price Index rose a seasonally adjusted 0.4% in October after 0.6% in September, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on Tuesday.

More2 sources
Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisRent, groceries and eating out are where a tighter budget feels this first — and a hotter-than-expected print makes a Fed rate increase next month more likely, which would push up the cost of credit cards and loans.

Prices excluding food and energy rose 0.3% for a third straight month. Food rose 0.5% (beef 0.9%, snacks 1.0%), energy 0.7% and electricity 0.9%; shelter rose 0.3% and accounted for more than 30% of the monthly increase, while used cars fell 1.2%. BLS did not publish a 12-month inflation rate because it could not collect October 2025 data during last year’s government shutdown. CNBC reports economists had expected 0.3% overall and 0.2% for core, and that traders now fully price in a quarter-point Federal Reserve rate increase at the Dec. 8–9 meeting.

Note: Market expectations for the Fed are CNBC’s reporting of CME FedWatch pricing, not a Fed decision; the Fed meets Dec. 8–9.

Hong Kong approves funding for the second part of the Northern Link, putting the full HK$86.2 billion rail line into construction

Official statementHKSAR Government press release, RTHK · Nov 10 (HK time)

The Executive Council approved the financial arrangement for Part 2 of the Northern Link on Tuesday, and the government and MTR Corporation signed the project agreement for the full line, the government said.

More2 sources
Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisThe line is the transport spine of the Northern Metropolis and a new rail link into Shenzhen — but the 2034 date means a decade of construction, and the MTR told RTHK it is its most expensive project.

The main line (10.7 km, five stations, linking the Tuen Ma and East Rail lines) and a 6-km spur line to Shenzhen’s Huanggang Port are now both in the construction stage and are targeted to open together by 2034 or earlier. Part 2 is estimated at about HK$54.8 billion and the whole project at about HK$86.2 billion (July 2025 prices), which the government says is 28% below the original estimate. The MTR will receive eight development sites, with a fixed sum deducted from their land premiums to help fund the work. Peak travel between Kam Sheung Road and Kwu Tung is expected to fall from 60 to 12 minutes.

Reported Reported by the linked news outlets; attributed to them, not independently confirmed by Donna Daily.

Official statement A government, official body or company's own statement, figures or proposal, attributed to it.

Claim An assertion by a person, government or group that hasn't been independently confirmed.

Allegation Claimed, charged or argued in court; not proven.

Unnamed sources Rests partly on sources the outlet did not name.

State media Relies on state-controlled media (e.g. Xinhua, CCTV, Russian state media).
