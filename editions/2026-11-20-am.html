Russia hits three Kyiv bridges in one of the war’s largest missile attacks, days after Ukraine’s Crimean Bridge strikes

Official statementClaimBBC News, Al Jazeera and 2 more · Nov 20 (Crimean Bridge strikes covered Nov 17 and Nov 19)

Ballistic missiles hit three bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv overnight into Friday, Nov. 20, and at least eight people were killed across Ukraine, officials said.

Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisThe strikes show how quickly the war escalates around symbolic targets: Ukraine hit the bridge Putin built to Crimea, and Russia answered by cutting the bridges that hold Kyiv together. They also landed in the middle of new peace diplomacy — European leaders say they are ready for talks and U.S. envoy Jared Kushner says Moscow may accept NATO-style guarantees for Ukraine — which the Kremlin has pushed back on. Any Russian weapon landing in Poland, a NATO member, raises the stakes further.
Full story, sources & checks4 sources

Russia struck Ukraine overnight into Friday, Nov. 20, with what Ukraine’s air force said were almost 100 missiles and about 400 drones, one of the largest attacks since the 2022 invasion, the BBC reported. Ballistic missiles hit three bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv — the Podilskyi, Darnytskyi and Southern bridges — which were closed or restricted to traffic, and Mayor Vitali Klitschko urged people to avoid driving into the city. Casualty figures rose through the morning: CBC, citing Kyiv authorities, first reported three dead in the capital; Al Jazeera and the BBC, citing Prime Minister Serhiy Koretsky, reported eight killed nationwide (six in Kyiv, according to Al Jazeera) and 28 injured. Strikes also hit railway stations, including one in Lviv where the mayor said two people died, and power generation, causing outages in Kyiv and other regions. Russia’s defence ministry said it targeted bridges used to move weapons. The attack came hours after Vladimir Putin vowed to respond to Ukraine’s strikes this week on the Crimean (Kerch) Bridge. Poland’s defence minister said Russian flying objects entered Polish airspace; the BBC quoted him saying a Russian cruise missile landed about 50 km inside Poland, while DW reported a flying object crashed in the Lublin region.

More2 sources
Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisHousing is Hong Kong’s most stubborn social problem, and the waiting time is the government’s headline measure of progress. Official statement A government, official body or company's own statement, figures or proposal, attributed to it.
