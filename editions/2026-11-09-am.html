Official figuresCanadian Heritage (news release), The Globe and Mail (subscriber) · Nov 8

More2 sources
Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisFor Canadian actors, crews and musicians, this is bridge money: it keeps productions financed this year, but the bigger fight — whether Netflix and other streamers must pay into Canadian content — is still unresolved.

Culture Minister Marc Miller announced on Sunday how $600 million promised to Canada’s screen and music sectors this fiscal year will flow through six existing funds and agencies. The Globe and Mail reports the Canada Media Fund gets the largest share, $213.4 million, with $68.5 million for Telefilm Canada, $67 million for music through the Canada Music Fund, $51 million for the Indigenous Screen Office, $10 million for Northern Aboriginal Broadcasting and up to $5 million for the Canada History Fund; independent production and local-news funds share $185.1 million. The Globe notes the package was first promised in June, after the government asked the CRTC to pause a rule requiring foreign streamers to contribute 15% of Canadian revenue, and that industry groups have warned it is no substitute for enforceable obligations on streamers.

A Development Bureau paper proposes expanding the campus zone of the planned Ta Kwu Ling university town from 40 to 75 hectares, reserving about seven hectares for a second campus of the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts. China’s factory-gate prices rise 5.7% on energy costs while consumer inflation stays at 0.7%

Official figuresState mediaNational Bureau of Statistics of China, Xinhua (state media) · Nov 9

China’s producer price index rose 5.7% from a year earlier in October, up from 5% in September, while consumer prices rose 0.7%, official data released on Monday show.

More3 sources
Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisRising factory-gate prices end years of deflation pressure for Chinese industry, but if energy costs keep feeding through while consumer demand stays weak, manufacturers’ margins — and the prices of Chinese goods abroad — will be squeezed.

China’s producer price index rose 5.7% from a year earlier in October, up from 5% in September, while consumer prices rose 0.7%, official data released on Monday show. The National Bureau of Statistics says mining prices jumped 27.9% and input prices paid by manufacturers rose 7.9%. Consumer prices were flat from September: food fell 2.9% year on year, led by a 16.9% drop in fresh vegetables and an 8.6% fall in pork, while non-food prices rose 1.5%. State news agency Xinhua quotes an NBS statistician attributing the moves to imported inflation and domestic demand, with gasoline up 22.4% and AI-driven demand lifting prices of data-storage equipment, tablets, computers and phones.

Note: Figures are official NBS data. Xinhua (state media) reports egg prices up 12.3% year on year; the NBS release says 10.1%. Explanations for the price moves come from officials.

Israel’s President Isaac Herzog formally handed Yashar party leader Gadi Eisenkot the mandate to form a government on Sunday, giving him 28 days, with a possible 14-day extension. Official statementCBC News, York University · Nov 8

Toronto’s York University says an “unauthorized actor” stole personal information submitted through some departmental online forms between 2007 and October 2026.

More2 sources
Why it matters · Donna Daily analysisYears-old forms that nobody thinks about are a common weak point; the breach is a reminder for students anywhere that sensitive information handed over for a one-off survey can sit online for decades.

Toronto’s York University says an “unauthorized actor” stole personal information submitted through some departmental online forms between 2007 and October 2026. The forms were used for things like residence applications, meeting registrations and surveys across 14 university websites, from the Centre for Refugee Studies to the Schulich School of Business. York says most of the stolen data was basic contact details and student ID numbers, but its notice lists categories such as health information, disability status and, for some, sexual orientation. York found the incident on Oct. 25, says its investigation found no sign the intruder reached other systems, and has reported it to police and Ontario’s privacy commissioner.

Note: York has not said how the intrusion happened or how many people were affected.

Official statement A government, official body or company's own statement, figures or proposal, attributed to it.

Claim An assertion by a person, government or group that hasn't been independently confirmed.

Allegation Claimed, charged or argued in court; not proven.

Unnamed sources Rests partly on sources the outlet did not name.

State media Relies on state-controlled media (e.g. Xinhua, CCTV, Russian state media).
