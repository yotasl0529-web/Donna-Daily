Last call: NYC early voting closes at 5 p.m. today; Election Day is Tuesday

FactUS · Politics & current affairsNov 1, 2026

Today is the ninth and final day of early voting in New York City, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., at your assigned early-voting site, which can differ from your Election Day site. On Tuesday, Nov 3, polls open 6 a.m. to 9 p.m., and anyone in line by 9 p.m. can still vote. Mail and absentee ballots must be postmarked or dropped off by Nov 3.
